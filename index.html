Geopolitical Lean-In Lab

Hold a stock/bond mix in normal months. When the threat index was high at the end of the previous month, switch for one month into the S&P 500 or into the long-only high-GPT-beta portfolio (P5, "long-only GHML") at the equity weight you choose. Every chart updates as you move the controls.

Inspired by our paper “The Pricing of Geopolitical Tensions over a Century”. The geopolitical risk series (threats and acts) are from Caldara and Iacoviello (2022), “Measuring Geopolitical Risk,” American Economic Review.

How it is computed. Gross monthly total returns: S&P 500 (CRSP value-weighted index), long-term government bonds and the T-bill from the Goyal–Welch data; P5 = T-bill + excess return of the top quintile of stocks sorted on GPT beta (value-weighted). Geopolitical threats (GPT) and acts (GPA) from Caldara and Iacoviello (2022), monthly from 1900. A month is high when the index at the end of the previous month is at or above the cutoff; weights are reset every month. Average return and volatility are annualized (×12, ×√12); the Sharpe ratio uses returns in excess of the T-bill.

Read with care. Results are in-sample, before trading costs and taxes. With "Full sample" the cutoff uses 1930–2024 data, which a real investor would not yet have had. "Expanding window" uses only the index from January 1900 up to the signal month, so the signal is known in real time. In every case the "best" weight is chosen after seeing the data.
